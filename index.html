Material & Craft

Fabric first.

Every fabric in this collection is milled, cut, and finished inside the same manufacturing group that has outfitted major regional and global sportswear brands for more than two decades. Nothing is outsourced past our own floor — which means fit corrections, fabric trials, and finishing standards are set by us, not negotiated with a third party. We know what performance jersey should feel like at 34°C and 90% humidity, because we've been making it in exactly that climate the whole time.
